## S1 (rounds 1–2, 26 queries) and verdict
* First-hand: AI takeoff misses (reddit snippets, capped at 2); losses from estimating errors in general: "$10,000 job I accidentally bid for $5,000" and an underbid job (ContractorTalk), $18k division loss (reddit). S1 counts pass for the reframed problem.
* **Pay signal: contractors already pay for checking** — and that market is served:
  * human: US Civil Genix "Estimate Review / Bid Check" and "Change Order Estimate Review" ($99), Pearson CC bid review ("second set of eyes"), BidAccurate (second-estimator review), outsourced takeoffs $150–5,000+, offshore takeoff at ~USD 11/hour (ContractorTalk ad);

* Rule 2 (the exact check is already sold) now HOLDS; S4 fails (a non-estimator founder cannot deliver a credible concierge check within 10 hours per customer).

**Verdict: KILL.** Lesson: willingness to pay for checking exists here, but it is served by people with domain skill and by AI tools already. The Israeli BOQ lane is not reachable through Serper (Hebrew queries returned guides only) and stays a lane-B intake for the owner's conversations.
